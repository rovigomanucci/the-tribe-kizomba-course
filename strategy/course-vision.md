# Course Vision

Status: Draft

## Vision

Create Kizomba courses where students feel weekly progress while developing enough control to dance socially with confidence.

## Student experience

Students should leave each class able to name and demonstrate one new movement. They should also understand the technical skill behind it.

The course should avoid two common failures:

- Foundations presented as repetitive drills with no visible payoff
- Moves presented as choreography without transferable understanding

## Course promise

### Level 1

Learn a new Kizomba movement every week while developing the connection, control, timing, and confidence needed for social dancing.

### Level 2

Learn the signature techniques of Kizomba Fusion, including leg crosses, leg slides, leg lifts, pivots, suspensions, counterbalances, timing changes, and dynamic combinations.

## Desired outcomes

### Level 1 student

- Feels able to dance a complete song
- Maintains timing and full weight transfer
- Communicates basic pathways clearly
- Uses core Kizomba vocabulary without depending on one routine
- Recovers from mistakes

### Level 2 student

- Recognises the follower's free leg and stable axis
- Uses advanced-looking Fusion techniques with control
- Changes timing and direction intentionally
- Moves between circular and linear ideas
- Adapts vocabulary to music, partner, and floor space
