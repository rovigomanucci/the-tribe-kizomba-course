# Movement Library

Status: Draft

## Level 1 movement families

### Marca

Purpose: Establish pulse, balance, and complete weight transfer.

### Basic 1

Purpose: Simple in-place or compact directional foundation.

### Basic 2

Purpose: Side-to-side weight transfer and connection awareness.

### Basic 3

Purpose: Natural travelling walk and social navigation.

### Estrela

Purpose: Circular movement and orientation change.

### Leader's Saída

Purpose: Create an independent leader pathway while preserving follower flow.

### Follower's Saída

Purpose: Guide the follower into a prepared lateral pathway.

### Vírgula

Purpose: Controlled redirection and rotational recovery.

### Retrocesso

Purpose: Direction reversal and pathway management.

## Level 2 movement families

### Foot block

Purpose: Interrupt or redirect a free foot with controlled, low contact.

### Leg cross

Purpose: Guide a free leg across a stable standing line.

### Leg slide

Purpose: Trace or guide a free leg near the floor with light contact.

### Leg lift

Purpose: Guide a prepared free leg through a small elevated pathway without lifting the partner's body.

### Pivot

Purpose: Rotate around an individual axis with body-led preparation.

### Hook preparation

Purpose: Create a controlled wrapped or hooked leg pathway only after the leg is free and the axis is stable.

### Suspension

Purpose: Delay weight transfer while maintaining structure and clear recovery.

### Counterbalance

Purpose: Create matched opposing intention while both partners maintain structural responsibility.

## Required metadata for future additions

Every new movement entry should include:

- Style label
- Level
- Prerequisites
- Entry options
- Exit options
- Timing options
- Common errors
- Safety boundaries
- Suitable music textures
