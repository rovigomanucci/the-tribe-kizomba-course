# Safety Guidelines

Status: Draft, expert review required

## General rules

- Both partners remain responsible for their own balance.
- Movement range follows the more limited partner.
- Preparation must occur before acceleration, rotation, suspension, or counterbalance.
- Students must be able to stop a movement at any point.
- Pain, fear, joint pressure, or loss of balance ends the attempt.

## Leg crosses and blocks

- Contact the free foot or lower leg gently.
- Never force the knee inward or outward.
- Avoid striking, kicking, trapping, or twisting.
- Replace the foot before transferring weight.

## Leg slides

- Keep contact light and close to the floor.
- Avoid pressure across the knee joint.
- Maintain stable standing-leg alignment.

## Leg lifts

- Guide the free leg pathway rather than lifting the follower's body.
- Keep the range small until both partners demonstrate control.
- Avoid hooking behind the knee.
- Return the foot to a clear landing position.

## Pivots and hooks

- Rotate through available foot movement rather than twisting a planted knee.
- Prepare the torso and axis before the leg action.
- Hooks remain optional and should not be forced.

## Suspensions

- Teach delayed transfer before any inclination.
- Avoid deep dips in this course.
- Return to individual axis before continuing.

## Counterbalances

- No surprise entry.
- Match distance and timing.
- Avoid dropping body weight into the partner.
- Keep ranges small.
- Teach recovery before dynamic variations.
- Exclude or modify for unresolved back, knee, ankle, dizziness, or balance concerns.

## Consent language

Teachers should normalise short partner communication:

- Smaller range, please.
- No lift for me.
- Keep it close to the floor.
- Let us try the core version.
- Stop.
