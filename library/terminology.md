# Terminology

Status: Draft

## Kizomba

Use for the foundational social dance language centred on walking, weight transfer, connection, and musical flow.

## Traditional Kizomba

Use when distinguishing the Angolan-rooted social-dance foundation from later Fusion or Urban Kiz developments.

## Semba

A related Angolan music and dance form. Keep it distinct from Kizomba in course language.

## Kizomba Fusion

Use for Kizomba-based dancing combined with selected external influences and contemporary movement tools.

## Urban Kiz

Use for movement language associated with more linear pathways, open connection, sharper stops, pivots, tension changes, and syncopation.

## Tarraxinha

Use for controlled body and weight-transfer interpretation, often with compact movement and close connection.

## Saída

A pathway or exit concept. Specify Leader's Saída or Follower's Saída where relevant.

## Vírgula

A rotational or redirection concept. Confirm school-specific naming during research.

## Retrocesso

A backward or reversing redirection concept. Confirm school-specific naming during research.

## Contratempo

A timing or directional concept involving off-beat or counter-time action. Define the exact use in each class.

## Naming rule

When different schools use different names, record aliases instead of declaring one name universally correct.
