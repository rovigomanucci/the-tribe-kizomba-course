# Decisions and Open Questions

Last updated: 2026-08-05

## Approved decisions

### Programme structure

- Two courses, each running for eight weeks.
- Level 1 focuses on Kizomba foundations and social flow.
- Level 2 focuses on Kizomba Fusion techniques, musicality, and visual movement vocabulary.

### Teaching model

- Each class has one recognisable headline move or movement family.
- The headline move carries one technical foundation.
- Students learn entries, exits, timing options, and recovery rather than one fixed routine.
- Each class ends with a social-dance decision task.
- Faster students receive constraints, timing variations, alternative exits, and musical challenges.

### Level 1 content

Level 1 includes:

- Pulse and posture
- Full weight transfer
- Natural Kizomba walking
- Basic 1, 2, and 3
- Cha-cha rhythm and lateral action
- Estrela
- Leader's Saída
- Follower's Saída
- Vírgula
- Retrocesso
- Pauses and taps
- Basic timing changes
- Floorcraft and recovery

Level 1 Week 1 introduces Basic 2 before Basic 1. This gives beginners an immediate visible movement choice before moving into the more grounded Basic 1 feeling.

Level 1 teaches cha-cha as a core rhythm and lateral movement skill in Week 6, after both Saídas. This gives students enough pathway and connection vocabulary to enter cha-cha from a Saída and return cleanly to Basic 3 or closed flow. Vírgula and Retrocesso move to Week 7. Week 8 combines pauses, taps, timing choices, and the full social-flow integration task.

### Level 2 content

Level 2 includes:

- Foot blocks
- Leg crosses
- Leg slides and tracing
- Leg lifts
- Pivots and rotational leg actions
- Suspensions
- Counterbalance foundations
- Dynamic counterbalances
- Timing changes and Fusion combinations

### Safety position

- Students must not carry a partner's full body weight during beginner or improver counterbalance work.
- The leader guides a free leg pathway. The leader does not kick, hook aggressively, or force a lift.
- Every advanced movement needs a prepared entry, controlled range, and clear recovery.

### Publishing and video sources

- Class Markdown is the canonical source for videos embedded in that class page.
- The video reference library is a cross-course index and must match the class files.
- The generated `index.html` must never contain a manual video mapping that is absent from Markdown.
- Open Night and every Level 1 class include an instructor preparation video. Level 2 references remain research items.
- The guide does not track or display course-completion status.

## Draft decisions

### Open Night

- The Kizomba Open Night is a separate 60-minute introductory experience delivered before each eight-week cycle.
- It sits inside `classes/open-night/` but is not counted as part of Level 1.
- Its primary goals are beginner success, partner comfort, introduction to Kizomba foundations, and conversion into Level 1.
- The draft movement order is pulse and walking, Basic 2, Basic 1, one simple quarter-turn, and one timing choice.
- Basic 2 appears before Basic 1 to create fast engagement and low-pressure partner interaction.
- The exact timing, rotation frequency, and movement scope remain subject to live testing.

### Other draft decisions
