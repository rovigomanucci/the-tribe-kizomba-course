# Safety and consent

- Connection distance remains negotiable.
- Open hold remains valid unless the class objective explicitly requires another position.
- No pulling, pushing, forced leaning, or forced range.
- Both partners remain responsible for personal balance.
- Students may decline a partner, role, connection position, or physical correction.
- Teachers explain corrections verbally before physical contact.
- Movement range must suit the available floor space.

# Teaching-role responsibilities

## Leader-role teacher

- Lead the principal instruction when appropriate.
- Demonstrate the leader role.
- Observe leading, timing, movement size, connection, and floorcraft.
- Manage timing, transitions, and whole-room corrections.

## Follower-role teacher

- Demonstrate the follower role.
- Explain the follower experience and technical responsibilities.
- Observe balance, anticipation, weight transfer, connection, and range.
- Support students during partner work and provide a second viewing angle.

The teaching roles may be exchanged without changing the structure of the plan.
