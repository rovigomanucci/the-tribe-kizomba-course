# Level 1, Week 2: The Kizomba Walk

Status: Draft

## Student-facing promise

Travel with a partner without pulling, rushing, or losing the pulse.

## Headline movement family

Basic 3, forward walk, backward walk, stop and restart

## Technical foundation

Torso-led direction and stopping

## Prerequisites

- Week 1 weight transfer
- Comfortable embrace
- Basic pulse

## Core version

- Basic 3
- Forward walk
- Backward walk
- Stop and restart

## Progressing version

- Add one prepared entry.
- Add one clear exit.
- Repeat with a new partner and adjust movement size.

## Challenge version

- Change travelling distance
- Delay the restart
- Add one quarter turn

## Entry options

- From Marca
- From Basic 2

## Exit options

- Return to Marca
- Open into Estrela preparation

## Social-dance task

Travel only when space is available. Stop before reaching another couple, then restart with the music.

## Common errors

- Stepping before the torso communicates
- Leader walking through follower space
- Follower anticipating

## Safety notes

Avoid large backward steps. Prioritise floorcraft and personal balance.
