# Level 1, Week 8: Social Dance Flow

Status: Draft

## Student-facing promise

Dance a complete song using choices rather than a memorised routine.

## Headline movement family

Integrated Level 1 vocabulary

## Technical foundation

Transitions, floorcraft, and recovery

## Prerequisites

- All previous weeks

## Core version

- Basic 3 into Estrela
- Follower's Saída
- Vírgula
- Free exit

## Progressing version

- Add one prepared entry.
- Add one clear exit.
- Repeat with a new partner and adjust movement size.

## Challenge version

- Change order
- Use fewer moves
- Match movement size to floor space

## Entry options

- Any Level 1 movement

## Exit options

- Any safe Level 1 recovery

## Social-dance task

Complete a song using at least three movement families, one rotation, one Saída, one pause, and one recovery.

## Common errors

- Sequence dependence
- Ignoring floor space
- Adding complexity after losing timing

## Safety notes

Quality and recovery matter more than quantity. No assessment through fixed choreography.
