# Level 2, Week 8: Fusion Flow Challenge

Status: Draft

## Student-facing promise

Build one adaptable Fusion flow and perform it with different timing and texture choices.

## Headline movement family

Integrated blocks, slides, lifts, pivots, and counterbalance

## Technical foundation

Musical interpretation and adaptation

## Prerequisites

- All previous Level 2 weeks

## Core version

- Saída entry
- Foot block
- Leg slide
- Leg lift
- Pivot
- Cross
- Counterbalance
- Musical exit

## Progressing version

- Add one prepared entry.
- Add one controlled exit.
- Repeat with a new partner and reduce the movement size.

## Challenge version

- Smooth interpretation
- Sharp stop-driven interpretation
- Slow suspended interpretation

## Entry options

- Any prepared Level 2 entry

## Exit options

- Any controlled recovery

## Social-dance task

Perform the same movement family in three musical textures. Remove any element that loses control with a new partner.

## Common errors

- Performing the sequence regardless of music
- Prioritising spectacle over partner response
- Skipping preparation

## Safety notes

Every advanced element remains optional. Partner comfort and clear recovery override completing the sequence.
