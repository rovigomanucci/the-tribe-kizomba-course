# Level 2, Week 6: Counterbalance Foundations

Status: Draft

## Student-facing promise

Enter and recover from a small counterbalance while each partner maintains structure.

## Headline movement family

Static, linear, and side counterbalance

## Technical foundation

Shared timing and structural responsibility

## Prerequisites

- Suspension and release
- Stable open frame
- Clear recovery timing

## Core version

- Static two-person counterbalance
- Linear counterbalance
- Counterbalance and recovery

## Progressing version

- Add one prepared entry.
- Add one controlled exit.
- Repeat with a new partner and reduce the movement size.

## Challenge version

- Side counterbalance
- Counterbalance into walk
- Change distance while maintaining structure

## Entry options

- From open line
- From suspension

## Exit options

- Return to individual axis
- Exit to Basic 3

## Social-dance task

Prepare, enter, hold briefly, and recover together. No surprise counterbalances.

## Common errors

- Dropping body weight
- Bending from the lower back
- Unequal distance or late recovery

## Safety notes

Each partner carries their own structure. Avoid deep ranges. Do not use with unresolved back, knee, or balance concerns.
