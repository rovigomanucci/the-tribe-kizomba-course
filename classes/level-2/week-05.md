# Level 2, Week 5: Suspension and Release

Status: Draft

## Student-facing promise

Create controlled suspension without dropping, hanging, or taking the partner off balance.

## Headline movement family

Delayed transfer, suspended steps, small inclination

## Technical foundation

Tension, preparation, and recovery

## Prerequisites

- Stable stop
- Open connection
- Controlled pivot recovery

## Core version

- Delayed forward transfer
- Delayed backward transfer
- Compression and release

## Progressing version

- Add one prepared entry.
- Add one controlled exit.
- Repeat with a new partner and reduce the movement size.

## Challenge version

- Small supported inclination
- Suspension into pivot
- Musical hold and release

## Entry options

- From open line
- From completed pivot

## Exit options

- Return to individual axis
- Walk out together

## Social-dance task

Use suspension on a clear musical hold. Both partners return to their own axis before continuing.

## Common errors

- Leaning before preparation
- Collapsing the frame
- Holding breath and becoming rigid

## Safety notes

Do not teach deep dips. Keep inclinations small. Both partners remain able to recover independently.
