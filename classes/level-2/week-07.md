# Level 2, Week 7: Dynamic Counterbalances

Status: Draft

## Student-facing promise

Connect counterbalance, redirection, and leg action without losing the recovery point.

## Headline movement family

Counterbalance with direction change and leg action

## Technical foundation

Momentum management and exit control

## Prerequisites

- Week 6 counterbalance recovery
- Leg lift control
- Pivot control

## Core version

- Counterbalance with direction change
- Counterbalance into cross
- Counterbalance exit into walk

## Progressing version

- Add one prepared entry.
- Add one controlled exit.
- Repeat with a new partner and reduce the movement size.

## Challenge version

- Counterbalance into leg lift
- Rotational counterbalance
- Counterbalance into Saída exit

## Entry options

- From open line
- From completed pivot

## Exit options

- Return to neutral
- Exit into Fusion combination

## Social-dance task

Use one dynamic counterbalance per song section. Reduce movement if timing, axis, or frame becomes unclear.

## Common errors

- Adding momentum without recovery
- Carrying partner weight
- Combining techniques before control

## Safety notes

Keep ranges small. Avoid rotational counterbalance unless both partners demonstrate stable recovery.
