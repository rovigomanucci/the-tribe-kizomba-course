# Level 2, Week 1: Cross and Redirect

Status: Draft

## Student-facing promise

Create clean foot blocks and leg crosses without kicking or forcing the partner.

## Headline movement family

Foot blocks, leg crosses, contratempo exit

## Technical foundation

Free-leg recognition and weight clarity

## Prerequisites

- Reliable Basic 3
- Controlled stop
- Clear follower weight recognition

## Core version

- Follower foot block
- Leader foot block
- Simple cross and return

## Progressing version

- Add one prepared entry.
- Add one controlled exit.
- Repeat with a new partner and reduce the movement size.

## Challenge version

- Cross with direction change
- Contratempo exit
- Alternative entry from Saída

## Entry options

- From Basic 3
- From Follower Saída

## Exit options

- Return to walk
- Exit through pivot preparation

## Social-dance task

Use one block or cross per phrase. The leader must identify the free leg before creating contact.

## Common errors

- Contacting the weighted leg
- Kicking instead of guiding
- Follower giving body weight to leader

## Safety notes

Keep contact low and controlled. No force against the knee or ankle.
