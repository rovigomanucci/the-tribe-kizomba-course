# Level 2, Week 2: Leg Slides and Tracing

Status: Draft

## Student-facing promise

Use controlled leg contact near the floor while both partners keep their own balance.

## Headline movement family

Inside slide, outside slide, slide into cross

## Technical foundation

Soft contact and alignment

## Prerequisites

- Week 1 free-leg recognition
- Stable parallel alignment
- Controlled leg contact

## Core version

- Inside leg slide
- Outside leg slide
- Slide and replace

## Progressing version

- Add one prepared entry.
- Add one controlled exit.
- Repeat with a new partner and reduce the movement size.

## Challenge version

- Slide into cross
- Circular tracing
- Slide with direction change

## Entry options

- From a prepared cross
- From open parallel position

## Exit options

- Return to neutral
- Exit to Basic 3

## Social-dance task

Choose slide size based on partner range. Followers maintain the leg pathway without collapsing the standing side.

## Common errors

- Pressing into the knee
- Dragging the foot
- Turning the slide into a kick

## Safety notes

Contact remains light. Avoid pressure on joints. Stop immediately if the partner resists or loses alignment.
