# Level 2, Week 3: Leg Lifts

Status: Draft

## Student-facing promise

Guide a prepared free leg through a small lift while both partners remain balanced.

## Headline movement family

Inside leg lift, lift and replace, lift into cross

## Technical foundation

Stable axis and controlled range

## Prerequisites

- Leg slides
- Clear free-leg preparation
- Stable open connection

## Core version

- Small inside lift
- Lift and replace
- Lift into cross

## Progressing version

- Add one prepared entry.
- Add one controlled exit.
- Repeat with a new partner and reduce the movement size.

## Challenge version

- Lift from Follower Saída
- Syncopated lift
- Lift into pivot preparation

## Entry options

- From Follower Saída
- From open parallel position

## Exit options

- Replace and walk
- Cross and redirect

## Social-dance task

Use one small lift only after the follower is stable. Replace the foot before transferring weight.

## Common errors

- Trying to lift body weight
- Hooking behind the knee
- Follower jumping

## Safety notes

The leader guides the free leg pathway. The leader does not lift the follower. Keep range below the partner's comfortable limit.
