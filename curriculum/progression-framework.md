# Progression Framework

Status: Approved

## Progression dimensions

Each movement progresses through five dimensions:

1. Balance and weight transfer
2. Connection and pathway clarity
3. Direction and rotation
4. Timing and musical texture
5. Choice and adaptation

## Class complexity

### Level 1

- Individual axis
- Shared communication
- Closed and semi-open connection
- Circular navigation
- Simple timing choices

### Level 2

- Free-leg identification
- Controlled leg contact
- Rotational and linear transitions
- Suspensions and shared intention
- Counterbalance with individual structural responsibility
- Multiple timings and exits

## Student differentiation

| Layer | Expected task |
|---|---|
| Core | Execute the movement safely and on time |
| Progressing | Add one prepared entry and one clear exit |
| Challenge | Change timing, direction, size, or musical intention |

## Readiness principle

Students do not need perfection before progressing. They need enough control to preserve their own balance and respect their partner's axis.
