# Level 2: Kizomba Fusion Techniques and Musicality

Status: Draft

## Outcome

Students use signature Fusion techniques with control, including leg crosses, slides, lifts, pivots, suspensions, counterbalances, timing changes, and adaptable combinations.

## Eight-week map

| Week | Class | Main move family | Hidden foundation |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cross and Redirect | Foot blocks and leg crosses | Free-leg recognition and weight clarity |
| 2 | Leg Slides and Tracing | Inside and outside slides | Soft contact and alignment |
| 3 | Leg Lifts | Lift, replace, and cross | Stable axis and controlled range |
| 4 | Pivots, Hooks, and Rotations | Quarter and half pivots | Body-led rotation and individual balance |
| 5 | Suspension and Release | Delayed transfers and inclinations | Tension, preparation, and recovery |
| 6 | Counterbalance Foundations | Static and linear counterbalances | Shared timing and structural responsibility |
| 7 | Dynamic Counterbalances | Counterbalance with leg action | Momentum management and exit control |
| 8 | Fusion Flow Challenge | Integrated Fusion sequence | Musical interpretation and adaptation |

## Entry criteria

Students should demonstrate:

- Reliable weight transfer
- Basic 1, 2, and 3
- Leader's and follower's Saída
- Controlled stop and restart
- Comfortable open connection
- Respect for partner axis and range
- Ability to follow a movement without depending on a fixed count
